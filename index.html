Cheque Bounce Case (Section 138 NI Act) — क्या करें?

Cheque Bounce tab hota hai jab bank cheque dishonour kar de — “Insufficient Funds / Signature Mismatch / Stop Payment”. Is situation me Section 138 NI Act ke tahat kanooni karwai hoti hai.

⏱ टाइम लिमिट

  • Return memo date → Notice 30 दिनों ke andar
  • Notice ke 15 din baad bhi paise na mile → Case file

📍 कहाँ Case File hota hai?

Judicial Complex Panipat jurisdiction valid hota hai.

📄 Documents Required

  • Original cheque + Memo
  • Legal Notice + Postal Proof
  • ID & Payment Proof

⚖ सज़ा

2 saal ki jail + cheque amount ka 2x fine possible.
